Misty Lake Cabin

Rain sounds for sleep & study · live 24/7

Misty Lake Cabin is a YouTube channel with a continuous live stream of a small cabin on a misty lake: real recorded rainfall, soft distant thunder and a scene that follows the time of day in New York.

Watch the live stream: youtube.com/@MistyLakeCabin/live

Misty Lake Cabin Live is the small private tool the channel owner uses to schedule these live streams on their own YouTube channel. It is not offered to the public.
